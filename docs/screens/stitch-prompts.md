# Stitch Prompts

Stand: 2026-04-04

## Ziel

Diese Prompts sind für [Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/) gedacht und
erzeugen erste Design-Richtungen für die nächsten releasekritischen Oberflächen.

## Leitplanken für alle Prompts

- Marke: `clearn.ai`
- Zielgruppe: Schüler, Studierende, Berufstätige im DACH-Raum
- Sprache: Deutsch
- Ton: klar, vertrauenswürdig, modern, lernorientiert
- Stil: hochwertig, freundlich, nicht verspielt, nicht kindlich
- Plattform: mobile-first
- Look: ruhige Flächen, starke Typografie, klare CTA-Hierarchie
- Keine generischen Startup-Illustrationen
- Fokus auf Glaubwürdigkeit, Datenschutz und Produktnutzen

## 1. Landing Page

```text
Design a premium mobile-first landing page for "clearn.ai", a German AI learning app for the DACH market.

Goal:
- Convert visitors into App Store / beta users
- Build trust for an AI-powered education product

Audience:
- Students, university learners, professionals
- German-speaking users in Germany, Austria, Switzerland

Core promise:
- "Foto zu Wissen in Sekunden"
- Capture notes or study material, turn them into flashcards, learn with spaced repetition

Sections to include:
- Hero with strong headline, subheadline and App Store / Beta CTA
- Three-step product explanation: Erfassen, Strukturieren, Verankern
- Key feature section: Kamera, OCR, KI-Karten, FSRS, PDF-Import
- Trust section: Datenschutz, sichere Anmeldung, klare Kontrolle über Lerninhalte
- Screenshot section for mobile app screens
- Pricing teaser with Free vs Pro
- Footer with links for Datenschutz, Support, Impressum

Visual direction:
- Clean, editorial, Apple-adjacent but not copied
- Strong typography, calm neutral background, blue accent
- Cards with subtle depth, no purple bias
- German copy

Do not design:
- dark cyber style
- playful student cartoon style
- generic SaaS dashboard look
```

## 2. Support Page

```text
Design a support page for "clearn.ai", a German AI flashcard app.

Goal:
- Give App Store reviewers and users a clear support destination
- Reduce uncertainty and support friction

Include:
- Hero with "Wir helfen dir weiter"
- Quick contact block with support email
- FAQ sections for:
  - Anmeldung und Konto
  - Käufe und Abo
  - Scan, PDF und Import
  - Lernen und Synchronisierung
  - Datenschutz und Konto-Löschung
- Small system status / response time hint
- Footer with Datenschutz and Impressum links

Visual style:
- Simple, trustworthy, calm
- Minimal support-center aesthetic
- Mobile-first
- German copy
- No corporate enterprise helpdesk look
```

## 3. Privacy / Datenschutz Page

```text
Design a privacy page for "clearn.ai", a German learning app with OCR, AI processing and optional paid subscriptions.

Goal:
- Communicate trust, seriousness and clarity
- Feel legally solid without looking cold or hostile

Include:
- Intro section explaining what data is collected and why
- Sections for:
  - Konto und Anmeldung
  - Lerninhalte und Scans
  - Zahlungen und RevenueCat
  - Push-Benachrichtigungen
  - Werbung / Rewarded Ads
  - Rechte der Nutzer
  - Kontakt / Verantwortlicher
- Clear CTA or inline navigation for "Support kontaktieren"

Visual style:
- Editorial legal page with strong hierarchy
- Excellent readability
- Calm spacing and typographic rhythm
- German copy
- Avoid law-firm stiffness and avoid startup fluff
```

## 4. App Store Screenshot System

```text
Create a cohesive App Store screenshot design system for "clearn.ai".

Goal:
- Communicate the product in 5-6 mobile screenshots
- Match a premium German education app

Screens to design:
- Auth / Einstieg
- Scan mit Kamera oder PDF
- Generierte Karten
- Lernen mit Spaced Repetition
- Bibliothek / Decks
- Pro / Paywall

Visual direction:
- Portrait mobile screenshots
- Clear headline overlay per screen in German
- Minimal chrome, strong contrast, premium look
- Consistent brand colors and typography
- No gaming style, no cartoon style
```

## 5. In-App Legal / Settings Concept

```text
Design a mobile settings and account screen for "clearn.ai" that is App Store ready.

Goal:
- Show account, subscription, notifications, language, privacy and support in one coherent screen

Required items:
- Email / account identity
- Subscription status
- Reminder settings
- Language selector
- Datenschutz
- Support
- Konto löschen
- Abmelden

Style:
- Native-feeling iOS-first settings design
- Premium and calm
- German copy
- Clear destructive styling for account deletion
```
